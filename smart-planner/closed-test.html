Help us test Feathly before launch.

We are inviting Android users to try Feathly: Smart Planner before its Google Play release. Stay opted in for about 14 days, use the app naturally, and tell us if something is confusing or does not work as expected.

Testing is simple

You do not need to spend a long time testing every day. A few minutes of normal use when it fits your study, routine, or work is enough.

  • Create a Loop for something you want to remember or repeat.
  • Try Recall reminders when they appear.
  • Try the Focus Timer.
  • Tell us about bugs, confusing screens, or useful improvements.

What we ask from testers

  • Please remain opted in to the Google Play Closed Test for about 14 days.
  • You do not have to open the app every day, but real use and feedback are very helpful.
  • We may ask for a short check-in around Day 7 and a final review around Day 14.
  • Do not share passwords, card details, recovery codes, or other sensitive account information with us.

Android devices

Feathly can be installed on Android 7.0 or later. Android 10 or later is recommended.

  • Android 12–17 users are especially welcome.
  • Older Android 7–9 devices are also useful for compatibility checks.
  • Samsung Galaxy, Google Pixel, Xiaomi/Redmi, OPPO/OnePlus, and other Android devices are welcome.

What happens after you apply?

Your application is sent to Feathly support and you receive an automatic confirmation email. The confirmation includes the Feathly Closed Testers Google Group link. Join the group using the same Google account you will use for the Play Store test.

Joining the Google Group does not start the 14-day period. The test period starts after you use the Google Play Closed Test opt-in link and become a tester.
