Data Deletion

How to remove Feathly: Smart Planner data and request deletion of data controlled by Feathly.

No Feathly account is required in the current version. Core planner data is stored locally on your device. Feathly does not currently provide a cloud account or cloud sync for Loop content.

1. Delete local planner data

You can remove local Feathly data in any of these ways:

  • Delete individual Loops inside the app.
  • Clear Feathly app data from Android system settings.
  • Uninstall Feathly: Smart Planner from your device.

Local planner data may include Loop titles, notes, schedules, reminder history, review records, settings, and other planner information stored on your device. If you uninstall or clear app data without first exporting or backing up information you want to keep, that local data may be permanently lost.

2. Request deletion of data held by Feathly

Feathly may hold limited server-side or support information when you use purchase verification, Restore Purchase, or contact support. To request deletion of data that Feathly controls, use the dedicated privacy request below.

Request data deletion

You can also email support@feathly.com with the subject Data deletion request.

3. Information that may be deleted

Depending on what information Feathly holds and what can be reasonably matched to your request, deletion may include support correspondence, support attachments, diagnostic information you submitted to support, and other server-side personal data that Feathly controls and no longer needs to retain.

4. Information that may be retained

Some records may be retained where reasonably necessary for purchase entitlement, refunds, fraud or abuse prevention, security, accounting, dispute resolution, or legal compliance. This may include limited billing or transaction records. Records retained for these purposes are kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for the applicable purpose or as required by law.

5. Google Play and advertising data

Google Play processes purchase and payment-related information, and Google AdMob may process advertising-related information. Requests concerning data controlled directly by Google may need to be handled through the relevant Google account or privacy controls.

6. More information

For full details about Feathly data practices, see the Privacy Policy.

If you have questions about a deletion request, contact support@feathly.com.
