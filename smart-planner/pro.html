Feathly Pro Lifetime

Current local PRO features for Feathly: Smart Planner. One-time Google Play purchase.

Early Supporter is a promotional badge for the initial period. The product name is Feathly Pro Lifetime. Early supporter price — one-time purchase.

What Feathly Pro Lifetime includes

Feathly Pro Lifetime unlocks the current local premium features of Feathly: Smart Planner, including ad removal and the local PRO benefits shown in the app at the time of purchase.

It is not a monthly or yearly subscription and does not renew automatically.

Cloud, server, and future features

Feathly Pro Lifetime covers current local PRO features. Optional cloud or server features may later have a separate small fee. Feathly aims to keep those costs as low as possible through efficient infrastructure and AI-assisted development.

Lifetime PRO does not include all future cloud, server, or AI services, and it does not mean every future feature will stay free forever.

Support

If PRO access does not appear after purchase, use Restore Purchase with the same Google Play account, then contact support@feathly.com if needed.
