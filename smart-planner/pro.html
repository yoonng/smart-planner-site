Feathly Pro Lifetime

A one-time Google Play purchase for Feathly: Smart Planner.

Early Supporter is a promotional badge for the initial period. The product name is Feathly Pro Lifetime. Early supporter price — one-time purchase.

What Feathly Pro Lifetime includes

Feathly Pro Lifetime is a one-time purchase that removes the bottom banner ad and supports Feathly development. All current Planner features remain free.

It is not a monthly or yearly subscription and does not renew automatically.

Cloud, server, and future features

Feathly Pro Lifetime does not include future Cloud, AI, or Account services. Those are planned separately and are not current Smart Planner features. Optional cloud or server features may later have a separate small fee.

A Lifetime purchase does not mean every future cloud, server, or AI service will stay free forever.

Support

If PRO access does not appear after purchase, use Restore Purchase with the same Google Play account, then contact support@feathly.com if needed.
